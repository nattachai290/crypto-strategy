# Lessons from 500 evaluations and runs (all coins)

_Written 2026-09-29 from `journal/_multi/meta_lessons.md`, which is generated by
`python src/meta_lessons.py` (it reads only recorded results). Re-run the
script after every round, and update this page by hand if a lesson changes.
§8 added 2026-10-01 after the open-interest round, and extended the same day
after the SOL/BNB replication of `051_retail_crowd_fade @30m` failed._

**Read this before picking an idea (AGENTS.md §1 step 1).** Each lesson has
the evidence in brackets. Rows are not independent: the 7 timeframes of one
idea, or one idea on 4 coins, are a single hypothesis. So the idea count
matters more than the row count.

**Caution first.** Most of these numbers are VALID results. Choosing the next
idea because a group did well on VALID is a mild form of tuning on VALID. Use
the lessons to **avoid what failed**. Do not use them to fit what worked.
Only the holdout decides.

## 1. Cost decides most outcomes: expect to hold for days

| avg hold | evals (ideas) | VALID > 0 | PASS+WATCH |
|---|---|---|---|
| < 4 h | 52 (17) | **0%** | 0 |
| 4–24 h | 50 (33) | 40% | 9 |
| 1–3 days | 156 (27) | 52% | 45 |
| > 3 days | 96 (16) | 68% | 53 |

- By cost per trade: at 0.1–0.3 R, 6% are positive (1 WATCH in 34). Above
  0.3 R, 0 of 20 are positive.
- Cost turned a gross win into a net loss in 21% of all evaluations.
- **Rule:** before writing an idea, estimate `cost_r` ≈ 0.0012–0.0014 / stop
  fraction (a 1% stop costs ≈ 0.13 R; AGENTS.md: a 0.5% stop ≈ 0.28 R). If it is above 0.1 R, do not run
  it. Nothing in that zone has ever reached PASS.
- Timeframes: 30m, 1h and 4h are positive on TRAIN and VALID; 1m–15m are
  negative on both, averaging over ideas. **Owner's decision 2026-09-29: 1m,
  3m and 5m are dropped.** Ideas run on 15m 30m 1h 4h (AGENTS.md step 4b,
  `tf_variants.DEFAULT_TFS`).

- **"Fade the crowd" and "follow the smart money" are mostly the same trade.** M2

  rises while open interest **falls** sharply (shorts forced out). Its short
  leg is −0.165 to −0.261 on BTC at every clock and −0.010 on ETH at 1h,
  while its long leg is flat. _Corrected in BTC Exp 038: this bullet first
  said "rising" OI, which is the opposite of what `oi_flush` tests._

  1.5–6% stops the whole round sat at or barely above its fee-only floor

  added about 0.002 R**, because the caps (48/144 h at 1h) are not reached. Compare the
  port round, whose invented 120 h cap was reached and pushed one config to
  0.151 R. So a wide stop plus a short cap is cheap here, and a wide stop plus a
  long cap is not.
- **The two biggest VALID numbers of the round were the two worthless ones**:
  M4 on BTC at 15m is +0.800 R on 29 trades that are **27 long and 2 short**, and
  +0.489 at 30m. Both pass the CI gate, both fail the trade-count gate, and both
  are **negative on ETH at the same clocks**. §3 and §9 again.
- **15m is the worst clock and 4h the best for the seventh consecutive time on a
  fresh idea set.** 15m is also the only clock above the 0.1 R line in this
  round (0.095–0.108).
- **Coverage asymmetry is a result, not a footnote.** Binance's top-trader ratios
  are **37.6% NaN in BTC's TRAIN and 80.8% NaN in ETH's, against 0.013% and

  ETH's while being tested on essentially all of VALID, and **M3 is untestable on
  ETH** — 4 INCONCLUSIVE rows, a 1h TRAIN mean R of +1.66 against VALID −0.11.
  **A negative replication row caused by missing data is not evidence.**
  Always check per-column coverage per split before reading a coin's
  replication, and never let a missing column be read as a null result.

## 9. Cross-sectional momentum did not survive either (2 runs, 104 weeks each)

Rotation (PLAN.md §17, `journal/_multi/experiments.md` Exp 000–002): weekly
rebalance of the 30 most liquid USDT pairs, ranked by past return, delisted coins
included. **spot REJECT, perp REJECT, 0 PASS, no holdout used.** Project total
**442** runs, 5 holdout runs, 5 FAILED, 0 CONFIRMED.

- **The spot top-fifth book lost to holding everything equally**: CAGR
  **+20.2% against the universe's +26.3%**. The perp book is market-neutral, so
  its +11.7% is not comparable with the long-only universe's +30.6%. Its own
  test is the statistic. Both VALID statistics had CIs spanning zero (spot
  [−0.0102, +0.0097], perp [−0.0067, +0.0119] per week), and both had a
  negative 2023 mean and a positive 2024 mean (52 weeks each). _Wording
  corrected in `_multi` Exp 003._
- **The effect is real before costs and gone after them.** Gross top-fifth
  premium **+16.0%/yr** on spot against **−12.2%/yr** of cost; **+12.5%/yr** gross
  on the perp book against **−8.4%/yr** of cost. **§1 again, in its sharpest
  form: at 1.5× weekly turnover the cost is the same size as the effect.** And
  the pre-registered rule picked L = 7 partly *because* it had the best TRAIN
  Sharpe — **the lookback that looked best trades most, and trading most is
  what eats the edge.**
- **The perp book's edge was mostly funding, not momentum.** Funding was **48%**
  of the book's gross (price + funding) and **74%** of its net. Without funding,
  the net is +0.00078 a week (about +4%/yr), indistinguishable from zero.
  _The first version of this bullet said 91%. Corrected in `_multi` Exp 003._
  Decomposition, mean per week: book price return **+0.00240 (+12.5%/yr)**, funding received **+0.00219
  (+11.4%/yr)**, transaction cost −0.00162, net +0.00297. **Shorts on the bottom
  quintile get paid while funding is positive, which is most of the time.** A
  market-neutral long/short book is largely a bet on carry, not on cross-
  sectional momentum — a different proposition from the one that was tested.
  `weeks_with_unfunded_positions` was **0 on VALID**, so this is not a data gap.
- **The survivorship rule cost real money, and the surviving-coin version of this
  test would have lied.** Delisted coins were held in **52 of 104 spot weeks**
  (0.69 of 6 slots) and **42 of 104 perp weeks**; their mean statistic was
  **−0.0105 vs +0.0120 on clean spot weeks**, contributing **−0.0053 of spot's
  +0.00075** mean. Removing them entirely gives spot +0.0060 and perp +0.0041 —
  still short of the gate, but **a study run only on coins that still exist in
  2026 would have reported a positive statistic, and it would have been false.**
- **§2 does not apply to a cross-sectional or market-neutral test, and that is
  what makes it worth running.** The perp book is long-short and the spot
  statistic is a difference against the universe; neither can be a long position
  in a bull market. **A REJECT here is a statement about momentum, not the eighth
  confirmation of §2.**
- **The sample size worked, for once.** 104 VALID weeks and 142–228 TRAIN weeks,
  against 100–400 trades in the per-coin rounds. The CIs are ±0.9–1.0%/week, so
  an effect of the size the literature reports would have been visible. **§3's
  "maybe +0.0 to +0.05" reading of a small VALID number is about noise; here the
  test had the power to see the effect and the effect was not there after costs.**
- **A prediction made in advance and wrong, recorded rather than dropped:** both
  the pre-registration and Exp 000 said the spot drawdown gate would be "nearly
  vacuous" because the spot statistic is market-neutral. **It bound hard —
  58.3% spot, 38.1% perp, against a 30% limit.** A market-neutral statistic still
  compounds with weekly volatility. The gate was right; the reasoning about it
  was wrong.
- **The data needed one code fix and one lesson.** `rotation.py` was the first
  code here to iterate every Binance pair, which exposed a
  `UnicodeEncodeError` in `datafeed.py`: S3 keys were interpolated into URLs
  unencoded, and Binance lists pairs with Chinese names (4 on perps, 1 on spot).
  **Any future tool that lists a whole exchange will hit it.** Symbol reuse also
  had to be handled: 15 spot and **52 perp** instruments are relistings of a
  reused ticker, including `BNXUSDT` used three times.

## 10. There is no skill in exits, and the structure is real but too small to trade (18 runs)

Exit lab (PLAN.md §18, BTC Exp 043/044, ETH Exp 009): **six fixed exits on random
entries** (p 0.25, side 50/50, seed 18), each entry simulated on its own with
the engine's exact rules — **test 14 checks all six trade-for-trade against
`run_backtest`, max |ΔR| 3e-14**. **1h REJECT on both coins; no holdout used.**

- **Every net mean is negative, on both coins, on both periods, for all six
  exits.** BTC VALID −0.079 to −0.150 R, ETH VALID −0.074 to −0.133 R, against a
  predicted −0.03 to −0.1. The best VALID CI excludes zero on the **wrong side**
  ([−0.1179, −0.0406] for the TRAIN-chosen `time_only` on BTC): the losses are
  not noise.
- **The mechanism is real and not tradable — this is the finding.** Gross R is
  positive for three of six exits on BTC's TRAIN and two on VALID, best
  **+0.0344 R** (BTC) and **+0.0543 R** (ETH), against a **0.05–0.09 R** round
  trip on a 13–28 bar hold. **Price has structure an exit can harvest; it does
  not survive the cost of harvesting it at 1h.** §1 in its cleanest form: the
  question was never whether price has structure.
- **A trailing stop is a cost failure, not a logic failure.** `trail_2atr` has
  the best gross R of the round on both periods, exits via the moved stop on
  **99.5%** of trades, and has the lowest turnover of the six — **and still loses
  0.098 R.** If a trailing stop cannot pay for itself on random 1h entries,
  nothing can.
- **Near targets are the worst exits, and the exit mix says why.** `tp_1r` and
  `tp_2r` fill target and stop about half the time each (51/48, 65/29) — **a
  coin flip paying 1 R against 1 R with two taker fees on top.** On random entries
  a target is a cost, not a strategy.
- **TRAIN chose a different exit on each coin** (`time_only` on BTC,
  `trail_2atr` on ETH) **and it changed nothing** — both are negative on VALID on
  both coins. The selection is not where the failure is.
- **THE 4H CELL PASSED ALL FIVE GATES AND IT IS A BULL MARKET** (BTC Exp 044).
  `trail_2atr` @4h: mean R **+0.3451**, CI [+0.0265, +0.7471], cost x1.5
  +0.3139 — **long leg +0.8140, short leg −0.1333**, while BTC rose **466%** over
  VALID. **A 50/50 long-short book on a rising market has a positive mean whatever
  the exit does, and here the exit is losing money on half its trades.** The
  identical exit on the same coin one clock down, same seed, same entries, is
  **−0.0983 R**: the exit did not change, the drift did.
- **And the generalisable warning (corrected in BTC Exp 045; the design flaw
  is the planner's).** A 50/50 random long/short book cancels drift **only for
  exits that treat both sides the same way over time** (a fixed hold, or
  symmetric targets). **A path-dependent exit converts drift into profit.**
  Under a trailing stop in a rising market, longs ride the trend while shorts
  are cut quickly. The mean is then positive from drift alone, and every gate
  can pass. So any future random-entry or market-neutral test must:
  - gate on the excess over a benchmark (as rotation's spot statistic did), or
    require **both legs positive separately**;
  - or run a control on a detrended path (subtract the period's drift).
  The first version of this bullet said the mean cancels and only the gates do
  not. The 4h mean, +0.345 R, shows the mean does not cancel either.

## 11. A trained model (LightGBM) did not find timing either (3 runs)

| ETH 1h | 13,266 | +0.011 | [−0.056, +0.084] | +0.075 | 3 of 6 |
| 20 coins pooled | 34,467 | +0.060 | [−0.037, +0.170] | +0.148 | 1 of 8 (the CI) |

- On one coin, the out-of-fold curve fell as the threshold rose: the model's

- **Rule:** a control that keeps counts and clustering but not volatility
  can be beaten by volatility selection alone. Gate on gross R, or shift the
  copies only within similar-volatility bars.

### 11b. Round 2 (smarter on the same data) was worse (`_multi` Exp 008/009)

- cross-coin and BTC features;

PLAN.md §22 (the owner's "candle pattern + location + support and resistance"),
BTC Exp 049 and ETH Exp 015: the new block `recipes.candle_at_level` (engulfing
or pin, at the previous completed UTC day's low/high or at a live 10-bar pivot),
tested at 15m 30m 1h 4h on two coins, 8 grid cells each.
**0 PASS, 0 WATCH, 13 REJECT, 2 INCONCLUSIVE, 1 UNSIZABLE. No controls were
run, because nothing reached WATCH.** Project total **456** evaluations, 5
holdout runs, 5 FAILED, 0 CONFIRMED.

- **The pattern really does carry information, and this is the round's actual
  finding.** BTC `055_swing` at 1h: **+0.1291 R gross, +0.0296 R net.** ETH
  `055_swing` at 4h: **+0.2240 R gross.** ETH `054_prev_day` at 4h: **+0.1047 R
  gross.** A candle pattern at a live level tells you something about where price
  goes next. **§1 is not "there is no structure" — it is "the structure is the
  same size as the cost of harvesting it."**
- **The 2.5-5 ATR stop floor is what made the experiment readable, and it is
  worth reusing.** A stop just beyond the candle would cost ~0.3 R and the gross
  number would be unreadable. Measured `cost_r` by clock, BTC: **15m 0.177-0.213,
  30m 0.130-0.142, 1h 0.097-0.100, 4h 0.041**; ETH is 15-25% cheaper at every
  clock (wider ATR, same multiple) **and still negative everywhere.** §1's
  prediction that 15m would lose was right in direction and optimistic in
  magnitude.
- **The cheapest clock is the illiquid clock, and that is the wall.** The only
  clock where cost is small enough (4h, 0.041 R) is the one that cannot produce
  100 VALID trades — 61 on BTC, 63 on ETH — and on ETH it also cannot be sized
  from a 1,000 USDT account at a chart-mode stop width (`size_skips` 1-2 on
  TRAIN, so both 4h rows are UNSIZABLE or INCONCLUSIVE and are **not evidence in
  either direction**). **Every other round in this project hit cost; this one hit
  the same wall from the other side.**
- **This is NOT §2's shape, which makes it one of the few clean negatives here.**
  Every one of the sixteen rows has a near-balanced long/short split (247/228,
  256/206, 170/177, 94/72) and **both legs lose**. There is no bull-market drift
  to remove and no need for a DRIFT reading. A two-sided family, tested honestly,
  is simply negative.
- **The two coins disagree on which pattern to use, and neither is worth
  anything.** TRAIN chose **engulfing in 7 of 8** files on BTC and **pin in 5 of
  8** on ETH. BTC's best cell chose engulfing and ETH's chose pin. §5's
  cross-coin disagreement, in its mildest possible form: one pattern is better

## 13. Not wrong-and-unlucky, not right-and-shaken-out: about a coin flip (stop diagnosis)

`PLAN.md` §23, BTC Exp 052/053, ETH Exp 017/018. The tool used every recorded
VALID trade (BTC 44,057 trades from 138 evaluations; ETH 10,738 from 58). It
checked whether price, H bars after the fill, was on the trade's side, and
compared that with random fills that had the same side, stop and hold.

| coin | real right | random right | direction skill (CI by idea family) | right-if-stopped excess |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| BTC | 49.0% | 50.5% | **−1.5 pts** [−2.7, −0.5] | +2.5 pts [+1.2, +3.7] |
| ETH | 49.7% | 50.2% | −0.5 pts [−1.7, +0.8] | +1.2 pts [−0.2, +2.7] |

- **The entries' direction calls are about as good as a coin flip.** BTC is
  slightly worse than random, mostly because of 15m and a 15m short-breakout

- **Stops throw away a little more than random**: about +2.5 pts of stopped
  trades ended on the right side. That costs something, but the entries have
  no edge for the stops to protect.
- **Rule:** wider stops or "anti-fake-out" stop placement cannot fix an entry

  on VALID.

## 14. Resting a limit order at support/resistance does not help either (4 runs)

## 13. A cross-coin confirmation is the only test that settles a lead, and it must be pre-registered (32 evaluations)

PLAN.md §26, `_multi` Exp 011: idea `057_coinbase_premium_follow` **frozen and
unchanged** on ten coins chosen by a rule fixed before any run (SOL, LTC, LINK,
BCH, ETC, ALGO, ATOM, DOGE, ADA, DOT), four timeframes each, plus
`baseline.py` on the 30m and 4h rows and `premium_confirm.py` once.
**`NOT_CONFIRMED`.** Project total **500** evaluations, 5 holdout runs,
5 FAILED, 0 CONFIRMED.

- **The 30m lead died completely, exactly as §8 predicts.** §25 found 30m SKILL on
  BTC **and** ETH. On eight unseen coins the 30m mean is positive on **2 of 10**
  and the control says SKILL on **1 of 8**. Bars were 7 and 5. **§8's "two-coin
  SKILL fails on new coins" reproduced on the strongest signal this project has
  produced.** The two coins that produced it had premiums that move together.
- **The 4h direction is real and consistent, and that is the honest finding.**
  **All 8 coins that ran have a positive 4h gross above cost**, and the pool of
  the 5 admissible coins is **475 trades, mean +0.2368 R, weekly-block CI
  [+0.1023, +0.3830]** - the widest, least coin-specific positive result in the
  project. **Both legs positive: long +0.2269, short +0.2481** - the reverse of
  BTC's own §25 split (+0.7047 / +0.1260).
- **It still fails, and it fails on breadth, which is exactly what a
  pre-registered bar is for.** 3 of 8 coins had **no eligible 4h TRAIN cell**
  (86, 94, 77 TRAIN trades against the 100 floor) so they are INCONCLUSIVE and
  excluded; 2 of the 10 never passed data validation (2 gaps > 3x the bar
  interval). **5 admissible coins against 7 required.** A diagnostic, recorded
  but not claimed: admitting the three INCONCLUSIVE rows would give 805 trades,
  mean +0.1515, CI [+0.0320, +0.2769], 7 of 8 positive - still not the rule.
- **14 of 16 random-entry controls are DRIFT.** Only BCH at 4h and ATOM at 30m
  are SKILL. **§2 again: what survives is drift.** The 4h book is in the market
  54.7% of the time and VALID 2023-24 is a bull market.
- **`train_positive_share` = 8 of 8 cells on TRAIN at 30m and 1h was a
  two-coin artefact.** On the new coins at 30m it is 100/50/25/38/25/100/100/88%
  - **median near 50%**. At 1h 75/88/38/38/0/75/88/50%. **A "positive in every
  cell" TRAIN profile is not a property of the signal; on BTC and ETH it was a
  property of two coins moving together.**
- **The 100-VALID-trade floor at 4h is unreachable for this signal on every coin
  tried**: BTC 72, ETH 86, LINK 95, BCH 91, ETC 97, ALGO 102, ATOM 90, DOGE 101,
  ADA 114, DOT 115. **Not a coin problem - the signal's natural frequency at a
  4-hour clock.** The same wall as §22's "cheap clock is the illiquid clock".
- **Method note, learned the hard way:** a coin's own premium and BTC's premium
  are *different columns*, and the cheap way to check a signal - rebuild it from
  the trade file and confirm every fill sits one bar after its signal bar - is
  only valid if `SYMBOL` is set. It read BTCUSDT bars while checking ETHUSDT
  results and produced 1,753 false mismatches. **Set the coin per process
  before trusting any independent check.**

## What to do with this

1. **Filter out, don't fit.** Skip ideas with expected `cost_r` > 0.1 R and
   holds under a day. Every other kind of idea is still open.
2. **Say what the idea does in a falling market** in the hypothesis, and
   check its short side. This earned its keep: the pre-registration asked about
   M1's long leg and the answer was that its **short** leg is the one that loses.
   Ask about the side you expect to lose, not the side you expect to win.
3. **The data sources the project had not used are now used** — open interest
   and the long/short ratios, 32 evaluations, nothing survived (§8). Two of them
   measured the same thing. What is still untested is **liquidations**, which
   Binance does not publish on `data.binance.vision`; `oi_flush` is the proxy
   and it failed. Any further use of the metrics needs a new owner decision
   (`PLAN.md` §14's stop rule).
4. Re-run `python src/meta_lessons.py` after each round. Add to this page
   only what still holds.
