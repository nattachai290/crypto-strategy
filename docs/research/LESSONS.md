# Lessons from 438 evaluations (all coins)

_Written 2026-09-29 from `journal/_multi/meta_lessons.md`, which is generated by
`python src/meta_lessons.py` (it reads only recorded results). Re-run the
script after every round, and update this page by hand if a lesson changes.
§8 added 2026-10-01 after the open-interest round._

**Read this before picking an idea (AGENTS.md §1 step 1).** Each lesson has
the evidence in brackets. Rows are not independent: the 7 timeframes of one
idea, or one idea on 4 coins, are a single hypothesis. So the idea count
matters more than the row count.

**Caution first.** Most of these numbers are VALID results. Choosing the next
idea because a group did well on VALID is a mild form of tuning on VALID. Use
the lessons to **avoid what failed**. Do not use them to fit what worked.
Only the holdout decides.

## 1. Cost decides most outcomes: expect to hold for days

| avg hold | evals (ideas) | VALID > 0 | PASS+WATCH |
|---|---|---|---|
| < 4 h | 52 (17) | **0%** | 0 |
| 4–24 h | 50 (33) | 40% | 9 |
| 1–3 days | 156 (27) | 52% | 45 |
| > 3 days | 96 (16) | 68% | 53 |

- By cost per trade: at 0.1–0.3 R, 6% are positive (1 WATCH in 34). Above
  0.3 R, 0 of 20 are positive.
- Cost turned a gross win into a net loss in 21% of all evaluations.
- **Rule:** before writing an idea, estimate `cost_r` ≈ 0.0012–0.0014 / stop
  fraction (a 1% stop costs ≈ 0.13 R; AGENTS.md: a 0.5% stop ≈ 0.28 R). If it is above 0.1 R, do not run
  it. Nothing in that zone has ever reached PASS.
- Timeframes: 30m, 1h and 4h are positive on TRAIN and VALID; 1m–15m are
  negative on both, averaging over ideas. **Owner's decision 2026-09-29: 1m,
  3m and 5m are dropped.** Ideas run on 15m 30m 1h 4h (AGENTS.md step 4b,
  `tf_variants.DEFAULT_TFS`).

## 2. The positive results are a long position in a bull market

| VALID, each evaluation once | long: mean R (evals > 0) | short: mean R (evals > 0) |
|---|---|---|
| BTC | −0.010 (56%) | −0.114 (15%) |
| ETH | +0.093 (78%) | −0.007 (47%) |
| SOL | +0.202 (88%) | −0.133 (14%) |
| BNB | +0.062 (69%) | −0.119 (8%) |

- The random-entry control said DRIFT on **102 of 108** WATCH/PASS results.
  The buy-and-hold benchmark said NO_EDGE on 101.
- 2023–24 was a bull market. Being long made money whatever the entry was,
  and every short side lost.
- **Rule:** a positive VALID mean R says nothing until it beats random entry
  and buy & hold. Before running an idea, say what it should do in a falling
  market. A "both" idea whose short side loses is a long-only idea.

## 3. VALID winners fall to about zero on the holdout

| config | VALID R | HOLDOUT R |
|---|---|---|
| BTC 022 @30m | +0.228 | −0.010 |
| BTC 038 @4h | +0.175 | −0.112 |
| BTC 039 @5m | +0.222 | +0.013 |
| SOL 041 @5m | +0.266 | +0.054 |

(The fifth holdout, `example_trend_breakout`, was used up while testing the
lock during repo setup.)

- 15 of 354 VALID z-scores clear +1.96. With no edge anywhere, about 9 would
  by chance, and the rows are correlated, so the real surplus is smaller.
- **Rule:** read a VALID mean R of +0.2 as "maybe +0.0 to +0.05". The best
  results were selected from many, so they carry the most luck.

## 4. TRAIN barely picks winners once cost is taken out

- Spearman(TRAIN, VALID) is +0.55 overall. **Among low-cost evaluations
  (< 0.03 R) it is +0.27.** Most of the overall figure is the cost effect,
  which is the same in both periods.
- A good TRAIN result is a weak hint, not a filter to trust.

## 5. Coins: related, but a result on one is not a result on another

- The same idea file has the same VALID sign on BTC/ETH/SOL 71–73% of the
  time, Spearman +0.30 to +0.43. On BNB it is close to random (37–51%).
- **Rule:** never carry a config to another coin without re-running it. That
  is already AGENTS.md §6, and now the evidence backs it.

## 6. No entry family has earned more tests

- No trigger family with at least 3 ideas is positive on TRAIN and VALID.
- The best single ideas each failed the holdout:
  - `momentum` is 1 idea (041), and it failed on SOL;
  - `donchian_break` is +0.037 per row but −0.032 per idea.
- Across 45 ideas, all from price and volume, no entry beat random timing.
- The one data source that was not price and volume has now been tested too
  (§8), and it did not break the pattern. **Nothing in 438 evaluations across
  four coins has reached a holdout CONFIRMED.**

## 7. Published TradingView strategies did not survive either (48 evaluations)

- Round 1 of the ports (T1–T6, BTC Exp 033, ETH Exp 007, review BTC Exp 034)
  ran 6 scripts × 4 timeframes × 2 coins:
  - **0 PASS**, 8 WATCH, 29 REJECT, 11 INCONCLUSIVE;
  - all 8 WATCH rows were NO_EDGE, and 6 of them DRIFT.
- The scripts' own charts (15m) lost to cost. Super Scalper: gross +0.004 R
  against cost 0.219 R. Liquidity Sweep: gross −0.066 R against cost 0.219 R.
  That is §1 again.
- The one SKILL signal (ChartArt MACD+SMA200 on BTC 15m/30m) did not
  replicate on ETH and leaned on 2023. That is §3 and §5 again.
- A script's author, its screenshots and its popularity add no information
  that this harness does not already test.

## 8. Open interest and long/short ratios did not survive either (32 evaluations)

Round M1–M4 (BTC Exp 036/037, ETH Exp 009/010) ran 4 ideas × 4 timeframes ×
2 coins on Binance's own 5-minute metrics: **0 PASS, 8 WATCH (4 SKILL, 4 DRIFT,
8 of 8 NO_EDGE), 9 REJECT, 15 INCONCLUSIVE.** The project total is 438
evaluations, 5 holdout runs, 5 FAILED, 0 CONFIRMED.

- **One cell replicated timing skill on two coins, and it is still not an
  edge** (BTC Exp 038). `051_retail_crowd_fade` @30m:
  - SKILL on BTC and on ETH, with the same parameters (but on ETH only 1 of
    the 4 grid cells was eligible, so that was no real choice: BTC Exp 039);
  - beta ≈ 0, and both legs positive on BTC;
  - VALID +0.106 R (198 trades) and +0.080 R (197), with both CIs and both
    alpha CIs including 0;
  - WATCH and NO_EDGE, so it is not eligible for the holdout.
  It is the closest the project has come. Only data newer than 2026-08 can
  test it honestly.
- **"Fade the crowd" and "follow the smart money" are mostly the same trade.** M2
  (all-account long/short ratio) and M3 (top-trader divergence) are two different
  columns and two different stories about who is smarter, and on BTC they agreed
  at every timeframe **by both being long**: M2's long leg +0.167 to +0.609
  against a short leg of −0.049 to +0.055, M3's long leg +0.251 to +0.805 against
  −0.089 to −0.263. The framing was not what was measured. That is §2 in a data
  source the project had never read.
- **Fading a short squeeze is a losing trade.** M1's short fires when price
  rises while open interest **falls** sharply (shorts forced out). Its short
  leg is −0.165 to −0.261 on BTC at every clock and −0.010 on ETH at 1h,
  while its long leg is flat. _Corrected in BTC Exp 038: this bullet first
  said "rising" OI, which is the opposite of what `oi_flush` tests._
- **Cost was never the problem and the pre-registration was right to look.** With
  1.5–6% stops the whole round sat at or barely above its fee-only floor
  (BTC VALID `cost_r` 0.035–0.108, median 0.063, floor 0.023–0.093): **funding
  added about 0.002 R**, because the caps (48/144 h at 1h) are not reached. Compare the
  port round, whose invented 120 h cap was reached and pushed one config to
  0.151 R. So a wide stop plus a short cap is cheap here, and a wide stop plus a
  long cap is not.
- **The two biggest VALID numbers of the round were the two worthless ones**:
  M4 on BTC at 15m is +0.800 R on 29 trades that are **27 long and 2 short**, and
  +0.489 at 30m. Both pass the CI gate, both fail the trade-count gate, and both
  are **negative on ETH at the same clocks**. §3 and §9 again.
- **15m is the worst clock and 4h the best for the seventh consecutive time on a
  fresh idea set.** 15m is also the only clock above the 0.1 R line in this
  round (0.095–0.108).
- **Coverage asymmetry is a result, not a footnote.** Binance's top-trader ratios
  are **37.6% NaN in BTC's TRAIN and 80.8% NaN in ETH's, against 0.013% and
  0.014% in VALID.** M3 was therefore trained on 62% of BTC's TRAIN and 19% of
  ETH's while being tested on essentially all of VALID, and **M3 is untestable on
  ETH** — 4 INCONCLUSIVE rows, a 1h TRAIN mean R of +1.66 against VALID −0.11.
  **A negative replication row caused by missing data is not evidence.**
  Always check per-column coverage per split before reading a coin's
  replication, and never let a missing column be read as a null result.

## What to do with this

1. **Filter out, don't fit.** Skip ideas with expected `cost_r` > 0.1 R and
   holds under a day. Every other kind of idea is still open.
2. **Say what the idea does in a falling market** in the hypothesis, and
   check its short side. This earned its keep: the pre-registration asked about
   M1's long leg and the answer was that its **short** leg is the one that loses.
   Ask about the side you expect to lose, not the side you expect to win.
3. **The data sources the project had not used are now used** — open interest
   and the long/short ratios, 32 evaluations, nothing survived (§8). Two of them
   measured the same thing. What is still untested is **liquidations**, which
   Binance does not publish on `data.binance.vision`; `oi_flush` is the proxy
   and it failed. Any further use of the metrics needs a new owner decision
   (`PLAN.md` §14's stop rule).
4. Re-run `python src/meta_lessons.py` after each round. Add to this page
   only what still holds.
