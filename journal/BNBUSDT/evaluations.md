- hypothesis: A breakout is only new positioning if aggressive traders keep paying for it. A close through the n-bar high on above-average volume says many participants moved; the taker buy ratio says which side paid to move it. That is why these are one idea and not two filters - flow without participation is a single trader getting filled, and participation without directional flow is a market-wide move nobody is fighting. Both directions, no trend filter, held for days.
